What is this?

This website is a place to lay some of my very old material to rest. It is based on a website hosted at NKU from about 2004. I've made the site look a little less dated, and freshened the text introducing the documents. But the content is still the old stuff.

All this dates from before my eighteen-year run as department chair and college dean. In some way, it serves as a "Before" snapshot (before my 2026-27 research sabbatical, say.)
